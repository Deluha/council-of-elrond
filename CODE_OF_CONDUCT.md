# Code of conduct

This is a small open-source project. We expect everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews, to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- keep feedback about the work, not the person;
- welcome newcomers and questions;
- accept that maintainers decide scope, and that "no" is a complete answer.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and sustained disruption are not tolerated.
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these expectations, and may block
repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the repository (an issue, or GitHub's private
reporting if the matter is sensitive). Reports are handled in confidence.
